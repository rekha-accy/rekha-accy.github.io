 I'm a PhD researcher in virtual machining at TU Dortmund University, with a master's degree in data science, also from TU Dortmund.

   My work sits where two fields rarely meet: physics-based simulation of machining processes and modern machine learning. Simulations are accurate but slow and hard to use, while AI is fast and flexible but often hard to trust. I'm interested in combining both, so engineers get answers that are quick, explainable, and grounded in physics.

   **Research interests:** physics-informed machine learning · virtual machining and digital twins · tool wear analysis with spectroscopy data · trustworthy and agentic AI for manufacturing

   I'm always happy to connect with researchers and industry partners working on AI in manufacturing. Feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or email.
